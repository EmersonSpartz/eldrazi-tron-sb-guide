Togores (Jul 2026) / Mengucci / Giltspire / retrogamer27 / Lucas Giggs / Cardsrealm · + full guide library below
Eldrazi Tron sideboards are mostly a Karn, the Great Creator wishboard (12+ one-of artifacts you tutor mid-game) — so most "sideboarding" is just 3 Disruptor Flutes coming in vs. combo. Knowing WHAT to wish for matters more than swaps; notes below cover that.
Togores' July 2026 list notes: Glaring Fleshraker is BACK post-Phlage-ban (2 copies — bridge tool; 3 possible). Off Sire of Seven Deaths earlier due to Solitude, but Boros/Prowess resurgence brought it back. 3 Torpor Orb (great vs. Blink/Boros; stops own TKS/Fleshraker — hence only 2 Rakers). No Chalice maindeck ("medium... better if open decklists"). Warping Wail down to 1. Basic Swamp re-added (Ponza). Breaker of Creation = only real Ponza tech.
