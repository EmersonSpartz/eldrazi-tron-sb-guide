Eldrazi Tron SB Guide

Mengucci / Lucas Giggs / Cardsrealm · + full guide library below
Eldrazi Tron sideboards are mostly a Karn, the Great Creator wishboard (12+ one-of artifacts you tutor mid-game) — so most "sideboarding" is just 3 Disruptor Flutes coming in vs. combo. Knowing WHAT to wish for matters more than swaps; notes below cover that.
vs. Boros Energy▼
Mengucci
Don't sideboard anything in.
Against creature-focused strategies (Boros Energy, Esper/Orzhov Blink, Domain Zoo) — no changes. Your maindeck is already configured for fair decks.
Lucas Giggs
Prose plan (no fixed numbers):
Favorable, BUT some lists now run maindeck Blood Moon — be careful with slow greedy Tron-assembly hands. Dealing with early Ragavan is crucial: want early interaction, fast Tron, or Sowing Mycospawn to ramp + block. Kozilek's Command shines. Post-board: Thought-Knot Seer is a weak Ragavan blocker (dies to removal/combat and gifts them a card — losing proposition). Bring in low-cost artifacts that answer Ragavan early and provide utility vs. hate/aggro starts.

vs. Izzet Prowess▼
Lucas Giggs
Prose plan (no fixed numbers):
More dangerous than Boros — faster clock, limited answers to Slickshot Show-Off. Early interaction is critical; stabilize with blockers and survive to your 7-drops. Post-board: Ulamog is simply too slow — cut it even though it hurts Ugin's Labyrinth ramp. Side in low-cost cards that buy time or disrupt explosive starts. You won't have time to fetch expensive Karn silver bullets — trim accordingly.

Lucas Giggs
Prose plan (no fixed numbers):
One of the TOUGHEST matchups. Explosive starts; your high-end threats do little to stabilize. Best route: early Karn, but you still fight Urza's Saga tokens + Pinnacle Emissary. Avoid slow/interaction-light hands — near-guaranteed to get run over. Post-board: Vexing Bauble excellent (esp. on the play — stops 0-drop turn-1 sequences). Cut Ugin + Ulamog (too slow) for cheap interactive artifacts. Disruptor Flute shuts down Saga tokens or delays Emissary/Kappa Cannoneer depending on build.

Lucas Giggs
Prose plan (no fixed numbers):
Not easy; play/draw matters a lot (early Amulet of Vigor swings it). Sowing Mycospawn much improved this matchup — exiling a bounce land early is game-changing. Thought-Knot Seer disrupts + pressures: clearest path to victory. G1: fetching Damping Sphere with Karn is excellent, esp. after Summoner's Pact when they lack basics to pay. Post-board: Ugin underwhelming; Ulamog CAN shine in long games (unlike vs. Belcher). Bring in disruption/hate, keep board pressure.

Lucas Giggs
Prose plan (no fixed numbers):
Combo matchups generally unfavorable, but you fight back: TKS = clock + disruption; Karn can shut Belcher off entirely via hate artifacts; Sowing Mycospawn exiling lands wrecks their fragile low-land manabase. Post-board: cut Ugin (does nothing) and Ulamog (too slow); bring in hate artifacts, keep early creature pressure. Delay their setup long enough to resolve a hate piece or push lethal.
